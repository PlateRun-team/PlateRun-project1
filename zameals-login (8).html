Privacy and legal

What ZaMeals collects when you order food, why we collect it, who we hand it to, how long we keep it, and what you can tell us to do with it.

Version 2.0 · Last updated 17 October 2026

ON THIS PAGE

1. Who this covers

This notice applies to anyone who uses the ZaMeals app or website to order food, to receive a delivery someone else ordered for them, or to browse kitchens without signing in. It is written for customers.

If you cook for ZaMeals or deliver for us, a separate notice covers your information; ask us for it at the address at the bottom of this page.

ZaMeals operates in South Africa and follows the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Where you use ZaMeals outside South Africa, local law may give you additional rights.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

Collected directly from you
Account detailsName, email address, cellphone number, password, delivery addresses, saved payment method and your notification settings.
What you writeOrder notes, allergy and preference notes, ratings and reviews of kitchens and drivers, survey answers, and anything you send our support team, including photos of an order that arrived wrong.

Information collected as you use ZaMeals

Collected automatically
LocationYour approximate location, and your precise location if you allow it in your phone settings, so we can suggest nearby kitchens and guide the driver to you. We track the driver's location during an active delivery so you can watch it move on the map. You can turn precise location off and type your address instead.
Order detailsWhat you ordered, from which kitchen, when, the price, the delivery address, the driver assigned, delivery time, and any refund or cancellation.
Device and usageDevice model, operating system, browser, IP address, language, app version, crash reports, which screens you opened and what you searched for.
Messages and callsChats and calls between you and a driver or kitchen placed through the app, and your conversations with our support team, including the date, time and content.

Information from elsewhere

  • Payment providers and banks, confirming whether a payment cleared or was reversed.
  • Kitchens and drivers, when they report a problem with an order or an incident at a delivery.
  • Someone who orders on your behalf, or refers you to ZaMeals, who gives us your name, number and address.
  • Fraud-screening providers, confirming whether a card or account has been reported.

What we do not collect. ZaMeals does not ask for your ID number or your date of birth, does not store your full card number (our payment provider holds that), and does not collect health information. Allergy notes you write on an order go to the kitchen and are deleted with the order record.

3. How we use it

PurposeWhat that means in practice
Running the serviceCreating your account, showing kitchens near you, taking payment, assigning a driver, sending order updates and receipts, and holding your order history so you can reorder.
SupportAnswering your questions, investigating a late or wrong order, and issuing refunds.
Safety and fraudSpotting stolen cards, fake accounts and promo-code abuse, and investigating reports of unsafe or abusive behaviour by a customer, driver or kitchen.
Improving ZaMealsWorking out which areas need more drivers, why deliveries run late, and which parts of the app confuse people. We use grouped, non-identifying figures for this wherever we can.
MarketingSending offers and suggesting kitchens based on what you have ordered before. You can switch this off at any time without losing access to the service.
Legal dutiesKeeping tax and invoice records, answering lawful requests from authorities, and defending or bringing legal claims.

4. Automated decisions

Some things happen without a person reviewing them:

  • Matching. Software picks a driver based on who is closest and free, the route to the kitchen and then to you, and current traffic.
  • Pricing. Delivery fees are calculated from distance, the time of day and how busy your area is.
  • Fraud checks. If a payment or ordering pattern looks like fraud, we may hold or cancel an order, or lock the account.

If an automated check blocks something and you think it got it wrong, email us and a person will look at it and tell you the outcome.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar identifiers to keep you signed in, remember your delivery address and settings, count how many people use a feature, and measure whether an advert brought anyone to us.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the ones we need to keep you signed in will mean signing in again on every visit.

When you close your account we delete it within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law, or information tied to an unresolved refund, claim or fraud investigation. Where that happens we keep only the part that is actually needed.

8. Your rights and choices

Under POPIA you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your account and the information attached to it.
  • Stop using your information for marketing, or object to a particular use.
  • Send your information to you in a portable format.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your details under your profile, switch marketing off under notifications, and turn off precise location in your phone settings. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days at the latest. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that nobody else can request your information.

9. How we protect it

Traffic between your phone and ZaMeals is encrypted, and stored information sits behind access controls so that staff only reach what their job requires. Card details are handled by our payment provider, not stored on our systems. Staff are trained on handling customer information, and access is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your information at real risk, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

10. Age

ZaMeals has no age limit. Anyone can open an account and order, and we do not ask for your date of birth or check your age at any point.

We do not sell alcohol, tobacco or any other age-restricted item, so no order needs an ID check at the door.

11. Legal information

Who is responsible

Why we are allowed to use your information

  • To perform our agreement with you — everything needed to take and deliver your order.
  • Because the law requires it — tax records, and lawful requests from authorities.
  • Our legitimate interests — fraud prevention, safety and improving the service, weighed against your privacy.
  • Your consent — marketing messages and precise location, both of which you can withdraw at any time.

Information leaving South Africa

Some of our suppliers, such as hosting and email providers, store information outside South Africa. Where that happens we use providers in countries with comparable protection, or contracts that hold them to POPIA standards.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. If a change materially affects you we will tell you at least 14 days beforehand by email or in the app. The version and date at the top always show the current edition.

Account and billing

How your ZaMeals account works, what you pay and why, when your card is charged, and how to get money back when something goes wrong.

Last updated 17 October 2026

ON THIS PAGE

1. Your account

One account per person, opened with your email address and cellphone number. Your account holds your delivery addresses, your saved payment method, your order history and any ZaMeals credit you have.

Keeping it current

You can change your name, email, number, addresses and payment method yourself in the app. Keeping your number current matters most — it is how the driver reaches you at the gate.

Closing it

Email us and we will close the account and delete what we hold, apart from records we must keep for tax law. Any unused ZaMeals credit falls away when the account closes, so spend it first.

Nobody from ZaMeals will ever ask for your password or a one-time code. If someone calls claiming to be us and asks for either, put the phone down and email us.

2. Ways to pay

MethodHow it works
Debit or credit cardSaved to your account for one-tap checkout. The card number lives with our payment provider, not on ZaMeals servers — we only see the last four digits.
Instant EFTYou approve the payment in your banking app. The order only reaches the kitchen once the bank confirms.
ZaMeals creditRefunds and promotions land here. Credit is used up first at checkout, and the rest goes to your card.

3. When you get charged

Placing an order does not charge you. Here is the sequence:

  • You place the order and your bank holds the amount as a pending authorisation.
  • The kitchen accepts it. Only then does the hold become an actual charge.
  • If no kitchen accepts within a few minutes, the order is cancelled and the hold is released. Banks usually release it within three working days, though some take up to seven.

A released hold sometimes still shows in your banking app for a day or two. That is the bank clearing it, not ZaMeals taking money twice.

4. What makes up the total

LineWhat it covers
Item pricesSet by the kitchen, including VAT. They can differ from the prices on the kitchen's own menu at the counter.
Delivery feeCalculated from the distance between the kitchen and your address, and how busy the area is when you order.
Small-order feeApplied only below the minimum shown at checkout, to make short trips worth a driver's time.
Service feeA percentage of the item total that covers running the platform and payment processing.
TipOptional, entirely yours to set, and paid over to the driver in full.

Every one of these is shown before you confirm. If the total at checkout does not match what you expected, nothing has been charged yet — back out and email us.

5. Refunds and credit

Report a problem within 24 hours of delivery and we will refund the affected items or send the order again. Tell us what was wrong and add a photo where it helps.

SituationWhat you get back
Cancelled before the kitchen starts cookingThe full amount
Cancelled after cooking startedYour delivery fee — the food is not refunded
Cancelled once a driver is assignedNothing — the delivery fee becomes a cancellation fee paid to the driver
Cancelled after the driver collectsNot possible — the order is on its way
Item missing or wrongThe full amount of that item
Order never arrivedThe full amount, including all fees

Refunds go back to ZaMeals credit immediately, or to your card if you ask, which takes five to ten working days depending on your bank.

6. Failed and double payments

A payment usually fails because the card has expired, the daily limit is reached, online payments are switched off for the card, or the bank's fraud check blocked it. Your bank will not tell us which, so check the card in your banking app first, then try again.

If you see the same order charged twice, one of the two is almost always a pending hold that has not cleared. Send us the date, the amount and the last four digits of the card, and we will confirm what actually reached us and refund anything that should not have.

7. Receipts and invoices

Every completed order produces a receipt in your order history showing the items, each fee, VAT and the payment method. You can email a copy to yourself from the order.

If you need a tax invoice made out to a company, email us the order number and the company name, registration number and VAT number, and we will send one.

8. Disputes

If you think a charge is wrong, email us before opening a chargeback with your bank. We can usually settle it in a day, where a bank dispute takes weeks and locks the amount up while it runs.

Where a card is used on ZaMeals without its owner's permission, we may be required to share the order details with the cardholder and their bank as part of the investigation.

9. Get help

Email platerun04@gmail.com with your order number and we will come back to you within two working days.

Terms and conditions

These terms apply every time you sign in, place an order or accept a delivery from ZaMeals.

Last updated 17 October 2026

ON THIS PAGE

1. Your account

ZaMeals is open to anyone, of any age. You need one account per person, opened with a working email address and cellphone number. Keep your password to yourself. Anything ordered from your account is treated as ordered by you, so tell us straight away if you think someone else has got in.

2. Placing an order

An order is a request until the kitchen accepts it. If a dish has run out or the kitchen has closed early, we cancel that item and refund it. Prices on ZaMeals include VAT and may differ from prices at the restaurant's own counter.

3. Delivery times and areas

Delivery estimates are estimates, not promises. Traffic, weather and load-shedding all move them. We deliver only inside the areas shown at checkout; if your pin falls outside one, the order will not go through.

4. Payment

We accept cards, instant EFT and ZaMeals credit. Your card is only charged once the kitchen accepts the order. We do not accept cash on delivery — every order is paid for in the app before a driver collects it. Any cancellation fee charged once a driver has been assigned is paid over to that driver, as are any tips you add.

5. Cancellations and refunds

An order can only be cancelled before the driver collects it. Once the driver has the food, the order is on its way and cannot be cancelled.

Before the kitchen starts cooking, cancelling is free and you are refunded in full.

Once the kitchen has started cooking, the food is not refunded. It has been made and the kitchen still has to be paid for it. Your delivery fee is refunded.

Once a driver has been assigned, nothing is refunded. A driver is assigned when your food is ready, so the kitchen is paid for the food. The delivery fee is charged as a cancellation fee and paid to the driver, who has already travelled to the kitchen for your order. The app shows you this before you confirm.

Report anything missing or wrong within 24 hours and we will refund or re-send it.

6. Allergens and food safety

Kitchens prepare food in shared spaces, so we cannot guarantee any dish is free of nuts, dairy, gluten, egg or shellfish. If you have a serious allergy, contact the kitchen through the order notes before ordering. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours.

7. Drivers at your door

Drivers deliver to the address on the order, and in complexes to the gate or reception unless the guard allows entry. Our drivers may end a delivery if they are threatened or abused.

8. Promo codes

Codes are for one account, cannot be swapped for cash, and expire on the date shown. We may withdraw a code or reverse a discount where an account has been used to claim the same offer repeatedly.

9. Your information

We collect your name, contact details, address and order history to get food to you, and we share only what a kitchen or driver needs to complete the delivery. We do not sell your details. See the for the full detail, or email platerun04@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what we hold.

10. Liability

Restaurants prepare the food and are responsible for its quality and safety. ZaMeals is responsible for the ordering and delivery service. Nothing here takes away the rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act.

11. Changes to these terms

We will email you at least 14 days before any change that affects your orders or your money. Carrying on using ZaMeals after that date means you accept the updated terms.

12. Governing law

South African law applies. Disputes we cannot settle by email go to the courts of the Republic of South Africa, and you may also approach the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud.

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask us most — ordering, delivery, paying, refunds and accounts. If your question isn't here, email us and a person will answer it.

For the full rules see the , and for what we do with your information see the .

ON THIS PAGE

1. Ordering

In short: pick a kitchen near you, pay in the app, and watch it come.

Where does ZaMeals deliver?

Pretoria and Johannesburg for now. More cities are coming — the checkout will tell you straight away if your pin falls outside a delivery area, before you've paid anything.

What times can I order?

Deliveries run daily from 08:00 to 22:00. Individual kitchens set their own hours inside that window, so what you can see at 08:30 on a Tuesday won't be everything on the platform.

How long will my food take?

About 45 minutes on average, from tapping order to the knock at the door. That is an estimate rather than a promise — traffic, weather and load-shedding all move it, and a kitchen with a queue moves it more.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum, but below the amount shown at checkout a small-order fee applies, so that a short trip is still worth a driver's time.

Can I order for someone else?

Yes. Put their address and a number they'll answer on the order. They don't need a ZaMeals account, but they will get the driver's calls, so tell them it's coming.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Only before the kitchen starts cooking. After that the items cannot change, and cancelling follows the rules below — possible until the driver collects, with a fee once a driver is assigned.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Not yet. Every order goes to the kitchen as soon as it's accepted.

2. Delivery

In short: we deliver to the address on the order — in complexes that usually means the gate.

Where exactly will the driver bring it?

To the address on your order. In complexes and estates that means the gate or reception, unless the guard lets the driver through. Adding gate codes and unit numbers to your delivery notes saves everyone a phone call.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Precise location makes tracking more accurate and saves you typing, but you can switch it off in your phone settings and enter the address by hand. Nothing else about the service changes.

Will the driver have my phone number?

They get a masked number that connects to you through the app, for that delivery only. Your real number isn't handed over.

What if I miss the driver?

They'll call and wait a reasonable time. If they can't reach you or get to the address, the delivery may end there, and food that's already been made may not be refundable. Keep your phone close once the order is out.

Can I tip?

Yes, in the app, and the whole tip goes to the driver. It's entirely optional — nothing about your order changes if you don't.

3. Paying

In short: cards, instant EFT or ZaMeals credit. No cash, ever.

How can I pay?

Debit or credit card, instant EFT, or ZaMeals credit. Credit is used up first at checkout and the rest goes to your card.

Can I pay the driver cash?

No. ZaMeals is cashless. Drivers carry no cash and can't take payment at the door — every order is paid in the app before a driver collects it.

When is my card actually charged?

Placing an order only puts a hold on the amount. It becomes a real charge when the kitchen accepts. If nobody accepts, the order cancels and the hold is released, though your bank may take a few days to clear it from your app.

Why does it look like I was charged twice?

Almost always that's a pending hold sitting next to the real charge until the bank clears it. If it's still there after a week, send us the date, the amount and the last four digits of the card and we'll check.

Is my card safe?

Card details are held by our payment provider, not on ZaMeals systems. We only ever see the last four digits.

What's in the total?

Item prices including VAT, the delivery fee, a service fee, a small-order fee if it applies, and your tip if you add one. All of it is shown before you confirm — the total you see is the total you pay.

Can I get a tax invoice?

Yes. Email us the order number and the company name, registration number and VAT number, and we'll send one.

4. When something goes wrong

In short: tell us within 24 hours and we'll refund or send it again.

My order was wrong or something was missing.

Report it within 24 hours through the app or by email, with your order number and a photo if it helps. We refund the full amount of the affected item or send it again.

My order never arrived.

You get the full amount back, fees included.

Can I cancel?

Only before the driver collects your food — after that it is on its way and cannot be cancelled.

Free before the kitchen starts cooking. Once cooking has started the food is not refunded, though your delivery fee is. Once a driver has been assigned — which happens when your food is ready — nothing is refunded, and the delivery fee becomes a cancellation fee paid to the driver for the trip they have already made.

How long do refunds take?

To ZaMeals credit, immediately. To your card, five to ten working days depending on your bank.

Something has come up and I need to cancel an order for someone in hospital.

Contact us and we won't charge a cancellation fee.

I'm not happy with how you handled it.

Email us first — most things get sorted in a day. If we can't fix it, section 13 of the terms lists the free routes open to you, including the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud.

5. Your account

In short: one account per person, 18 and over, closable whenever you like.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can look at kitchens and prices signed out. You need an account to place an order.

Is there an age limit?

Yes — ZaMeals accounts are for people 18 and over.

I've forgotten my password.

Use the reset link on the sign-in page, or email us and we'll send you one.

Someone else has been using my account.

Tell us straight away and we'll lock it. Anything ordered from your account counts as ordered by you until you report it, so don't wait.

Will ZaMeals ever phone me for my password or a code?

Never. Nobody here will ask for either. If someone calls claiming to be us and asks, put the phone down and email us.

How do I close my account?

From your profile, or by emailing us. Unused ZaMeals credit falls away when the account closes, so spend it first. We keep order and tax records for the period set out in the Privacy Policy.

How do I see what you hold about me?

Ask us — by email, WhatsApp, SMS or phone. It's free and we'll answer within 30 days.

How do I stop the marketing messages?

Turn them off in your notification settings, tap unsubscribe, or reply STOP. Order updates and receipts carry on, because those are part of the service.

6. Food, allergies and safety

In short: with a serious allergy, phone the kitchen before you order. Don't rely on the notes field.

If you have a serious or life-threatening allergy, contact the kitchen directly before you order. Kitchens on ZaMeals cook in shared spaces, so no dish can be guaranteed free of nuts, dairy, gluten, egg, soya, sesame, fish or shellfish — even where the menu doesn't list them. Order notes aren't always read before cooking starts.

Who is responsible for the food itself?

The kitchen prepares and sells it, and answers for its quality and safety. ZaMeals runs the ordering, payment and delivery around it.

Where does the allergen information come from?

From the kitchen. We pass it on as we receive it.

Are the kitchens checked?

Every kitchen must hold a valid Certificate of Acceptability from its municipality. We check it at sign-up and can ask for it again at any time.

What do you do with my allergy note?

It goes to that kitchen for that order and nowhere else. It isn't kept against your profile or used for marketing.

I got sick after an order.

Tell us as soon as you can. We pass the report to the kitchen and, where it's appropriate, to the municipal health department.

Do you deliver alcohol?

No. We don't sell alcohol, tobacco or any other age-restricted item.

7. Selling and driving with us

In short: email us and we'll take you through it.

I run a kitchen and want to sell on ZaMeals.

Email us. You'll need your business registration, a valid Certificate of Acceptability, any licences that apply to what you sell, and banking details for payouts.

Can a home kitchen or spaza join?

Yes, on the same terms — registration, certificate and licences included. That's a municipal requirement, not ours.

I want to deliver for ZaMeals.

Email us with your area and what you'd be riding or driving. We'll send you what's needed.

What can a kitchen do with my details as a buyer?

Only prepare and hand over your order. They're contractually barred from keeping your details, adding you to a mailing list or passing them on.

8. Still stuck?

In short: email us with your order number and a person will reply.

Email platerun04@gmail.com. Include your order number if it's about an order — it's the quickest way to a refund. We aim to acknowledge within one working day.
